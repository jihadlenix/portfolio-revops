The Mission

A migration either reconciles or it is not done.

Most CRM problems are not configuration problems. They are data problems wearing a configuration costume. A duplicate nobody caught, an association that silently dropped, a lead source overwritten on import. So I count the records before and after, check the identifiers and the associations, log whatever failed so it can go back in safely, and write down the mapping. It is slower on the first pass and much faster every pass after that.

Pillar 02

Migration & Data Quality

Counted in, counted out, and reconciled against the source.

I built a Salesforce to HubSpot migration platform in Python. It maps Salesforce exports onto HubSpot properties, standardises emails, phones, company names, dates and currencies, and flags duplicates and missing fields before anything is imported. It runs sample, full and delta imports through the HubSpot API with rate-limit handling and retries, and it preserves what usually gets lost: contact-company and deal-company associations, lead source, and consent data. After every run it reconciles record counts, identifiers, associations and key fields, logs failed records so they can be re-imported safely, and ships with a field-mapping document and a migration runbook.

  • Reconciliation
